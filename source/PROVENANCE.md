# Source provenance — verified 2026-10-02

All files in `raw/` are works of the United States Government (Department of the Army /
Department of Defense), prepared by military personnel as part of official duties — public
domain by 17 U.S.C. §105.

- `FM21-76_survival.txt` — FM 21-76 *Survival*, Dept. of the Army. OCR text from
  https://archive.org/details/Fm21-76SurvivalManual (item marked public domain). 558,889 bytes.
- `FM21-11_firstaid_1976.txt` — FM 21-11 *First Aid for Soldiers*, June 1976. OCR text from
  https://archive.org/details/milmanual-fm-21-11-first-aid-for-soldiers. 197,722 bytes.
- `FM21-11_firstaid_1943.txt` — FM 21-11, 1943 edition, from https://archive.org/details/Fm21-11.
  Kept as contrast source; not used in v1.

**Rejected:** 1992 Barnes & Noble / Platinum Press reprint of FM 21-76 ("New material © 1991
Platinum Press Inc." — visible on its archive.org scan). Any reprint-derived text is forbidden.

**Known OCR noise (djvu OCR):** `Tlie`, `firat`, `niny`, `KQ-1`, mis-split headings, occasional
`1 1` for `11`. The extraction pipeline must flag, not silently fix, meaning-critical tokens
(doses, ratios, times, temperatures, drug names).
